Delete Your Account — Sözbil

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Sözbil does not use passwords or email sign-in — your profile is identified by a random device ID generated the first time you open the app. You can request permanent deletion of that profile and everything tied to it at any time.

What gets deleted

When you request account deletion, we permanently remove:

  • Your player profile (display name, avatar, location text, device ID)
  • Your gameplay data (scores, XP, coins, streaks, achievements, leaderboard entries)
  • Chat messages you have sent

If you have an active Sözbil Premium subscription, deleting your account does not automatically cancel billing — please also cancel your subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store subscription settings on your device.

How to request deletion

Email canabdiyev@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Sözbil account". Include the display name shown on your profile so we can find the right record — we do not ask for or need a password.

We process deletion requests within 7 days and will confirm by email once your data has been removed.

Questions

See our Privacy Policy for more on what we collect and why, or contact canabdiyev@gmail.com.
