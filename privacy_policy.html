Privacy Policy — Sözbil

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sözbil ("the app," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. Sözbil does not require an email, phone number, or password. When you first open the app, we generate a random device identifier (UUID) to identify your profile. You choose a display name, an avatar (from a set of preset icons), and may optionally enter your location as free text (e.g. a city name).

Optional sign-in. You can choose to link your profile to Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In so you can restore your progress on a new phone. If you do, we receive and store the account identifier that Apple or Google issues for Sözbil, and the email address they share with us (with Sign in with Apple this can be a private relay address). We use these only to recognise your account when you sign in again — never for marketing. Signing in is not required to play.

Cloud save. When you are signed in, a backup of your in-app progress and settings (game progress, unlocked items, preferences) is stored on our servers so it can be restored on another device.

User content. Messages you send in the app's group chat are visible to other players and stored on our servers. Please do not share sensitive personal information in chat.

Gameplay data. We store your scores, game history, streaks, achievements, and virtual currency balances (XP, coins, gems), season-pass progress, profile frames, friends you add by code, and game challenges you send or accept, to power leaderboards and progression features.

Photos. If you choose to set a profile picture, the app requests access to your photo library to let you select an image. We do not access your photos for any other purpose.

Advertising and analytics data. The app displays ads via Google AdMob. Depending on your device settings and consent choices, this may include an advertising identifier used to personalize ads. See "Advertising" below. We also use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) to understand which features are used, to receive crash reports with technical details about the device and app state, and to adjust game settings remotely. These tools use your random profile ID and an app-instance identifier, not your name or email. See "Analytics and crash reporting" below.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to Sözbil Premium, your purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play — we do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive confirmation of an active subscription to unlock premium features.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to: operate core gameplay features (scoring, streaks, achievements, leaderboards); display your chosen name/avatar/location to other players (leaderboard, chat); enforce daily play limits and reward-ad bonuses; provide and manage premium subscriptions; show advertising that helps keep the app free to play; and improve and maintain the app.

3. Advertising

Sözbil uses Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and other data as described in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. On iOS, whether ads are personalized depends on your App Tracking Transparency choice, if presented. On Android, you can manage ad personalization in your device's Google Ads Settings.

4. Chat

The in-app group chat is visible to all players and is not end-to-end encrypted. We reserve the right to remove messages that violate community standards. Do not share personal, financial, or otherwise sensitive information in chat. You can report or block another player by long-pressing their message. Reports, blocks and the reported message are stored so we can review them; a message reported by several players is hidden automatically, and an automatic filter masks offensive words.

4a. Analytics and crash reporting

Sözbil uses Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config. They collect app-usage events (for example, which game was opened or finished), crash and error reports, device model, operating system version, app version and an app-instance identifier. This data is processed by Google as described in Firebase's privacy information. It is used only to fix problems and improve the app.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with: service providers who help operate the app (hosting, advertising networks, Google Firebase analytics and crash reporting, Apple and Google sign-in, app store payment processing); other users, limited to your display name, avatar, profile frame, location text, chat messages, leaderboard rank, and — for players you add as friends or challenge — your scores in those games — by design, since these are social/competitive features; and law enforcement or legal authorities, only if required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain your profile and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account), or by request — see our Delete Account page. Crash and analytics data held by Firebase is kept according to Google's retention settings (at most 14 months for analytics).

7. Children's Privacy

Sözbil is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Your Choices

You can edit your display name, avatar, and location at any time in your profile. You can sign out of, or unlink, Apple or Google sign-in, and delete your account in the app. You can request deletion of chat messages you've sent by contacting us. You can uninstall the app at any time, which stops all local data collection; your device UUID and associated server-side data will remain unless you request deletion. You can manage ad personalization through your device's OS-level privacy/ad settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, contact us at:
